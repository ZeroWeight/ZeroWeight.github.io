
\section{Research Interest}

My research focuses on machine learning, with an emphasis on \textbf{reinforcement learning} (RL). I aim to advance the theoretical foundations of RL and broaden its applicability including large language models, agentic systems, \textbf{embodied AI} and generative modeling. I am also working on developing \textbf{AI for scientific discovery}, particularly in the \textbf{self-driving laboratories} in chemistry using specific-designed RL algorithms and modern machine learning models.
