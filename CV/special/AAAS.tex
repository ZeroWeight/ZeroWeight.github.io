\section{Research Interest}
My research focuses on machine learning, with an emphasis on developing \textbf{AI for scientific discovery}, particularly in the \textbf{self-driving laboratories} in (electro)chemistry using specific-designed algorithms and modern machine learning models. Methodologically, I work on \textbf{reinforcement learning} (RL) and \textbf{imitation learning} (IL) to tackle the challenges in self-driving lab. I aim to advance the theoretical foundations of RL/IL and broaden its applicability to complex challenges, including large language models, agentic systems, \textbf{embodied AI} and generative modeling and self-driving labs.
