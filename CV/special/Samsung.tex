\section{Research Interest}
My research focuses on machine learning, with an emphasis on \textbf{reinforcement learning} (RL) and \textbf{imitation learning} (IL). I aim to advance the theoretical foundations of RL/IL and broaden its applicability to complex challenges, including \textbf{large language models}, \textbf{agentic systems}, embodied AI and generative modeling. % I am also working on developing AI for scientific discovery, particularly in the self-driving laboratories in chemistry using specific-designed RL/IL algorithms and modern machine learning models.
